I. The Story

A Louisville legend.

This historic building is where Carl Kaelin is credited with inventing the cheeseburger — a claim that's as much a part of Louisville as the Kentucky Derby.

Since 1934, the Kaelin's name has been synonymous with Louisville dining excellence.

Today, 80/20 at Kaelin's honors that legacy while bringing fresh energy to the neighborhood. We're serving up classic American fare — burgers, fried chicken, craft cocktails — in a space that feels both nostalgic and new.

Whether you're here for a family dinner on the patio, drinks at the bar, or a cone from the malt shop, you're part of a story that's been nearly a century in the making.

Now. 80/20 at Kaelin's — 1801 Newburg Road.
